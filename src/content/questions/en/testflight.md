---
title: "What is TestFlight and what is it used for?"
category: tooling
order: 30
---

TestFlight is Apple's service for beta testing apps before release to the App Store. The developer uploads a build to App Store Connect, and testers install it on their device through the TestFlight app and leave feedback and crash reports.

There are two kinds of testers:

- **internal**: team members in App Store Connect (up to 100); the build is available right after processing;
- **external**: any users invited by email or a public link (up to 10,000); the first build for external testing goes through a brief review (Beta App Review).

A build is available in TestFlight for 90 days. The service is used to check the app on real devices and iOS versions and to collect feedback and crash reports before publishing.
