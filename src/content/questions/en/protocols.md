---
title: "Protocols"
category: swift
order: 52
---

A protocol defines a "blueprint" of methods, properties, and other requirements suitable for a particular task. A protocol can be adopted by a class, struct, or enum, which provide the actual implementation of those requirements; a type that satisfies the requirements conforms to the protocol.

A protocol can be extended (`extension`) to implement some of the requirements or to add extra functionality available to all conforming types.
