---
title: "Which UIViewController lifecycle methods do you know?"
category: uikit
order: 22
---

1. `init` / `loadView`
2. `viewDidLoad`
3. `viewWillAppear`
4. `viewIsAppearing` (better not to mention it)
5. `viewWillLayoutSubviews`
6. `viewDidLayoutSubviews`
7. `viewDidAppear`
8. `viewWillDisappear`
9. `viewDidDisappear`
10. `viewWillTransition`
11. `didReceiveMemoryWarning`: can be called at any time
12. `deinit`
