---
title: "What is polymorphism?"
category: architecture
order: 3
---

Polymorphism is the ability of an object to use the methods of a derived class that did not exist at the time the base class was created.

There is static polymorphism (overloading and generics, where the implementation is chosen at compile time) and dynamic polymorphism (method overriding and protocols, where it is chosen at runtime).
