---
title: "What do the design principles KISS/DRY/YAGNI mean?"
category: architecture
order: 12
---

**KISS**: Keep It Simple, Stupid. Most systems work better without unnecessary complications, so simplicity is one of a developer's main goals: programs should be written without needless complexity.

**DRY**: Don't Repeat Yourself. The formulation: "Every piece of knowledge must have a single, unambiguous, authoritative representation within a system."

**YAGNI**: You Aren't Gonna Need It. A software design principle whose goal is to avoid excess functionality and adding things that aren't needed.
