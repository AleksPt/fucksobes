---
title: "What kinds of initializers are there?"
category: swift
order: 29
---

1. **Default initializer** — with default property values.
2. **Memberwise** — for structs.
3. **Failable** — can return a value or `nil`.
4. **`required`** — must be implemented in all subclasses of the class.
5. **Designated.**
6. **Convenience.**
