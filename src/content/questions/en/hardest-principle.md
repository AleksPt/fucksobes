---
title: "Which principle is the hardest to follow?"
category: architecture
order: 8
---

The hardest to follow is the **Open/Closed Principle**.

- **Planning ahead.** It is hard to predict how functionality will change, especially in the early stages of a project. New requirements often force you to modify existing code.
- **Balancing flexibility and simplicity.** To make a class open for extension but closed for modification, you often need abstractions (protocols, interfaces) or patterns (for example, Strategy, Factory). This can complicate the architecture, especially if that flexibility is not needed yet.
