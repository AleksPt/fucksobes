---
title: "What is declarative syntax?"
category: swiftui
order: 22
---

The declarative approach means describing **what** the result should be, not **how** to get there step by step. The developer describes the interface for the current state of the data, and SwiftUI decides how to render it and update it when things change.

For example, you write that there should be an `Image` at the bottom of the screen, and you don't worry about creating an object, calculating its frame, or adding it to the hierarchy by hand — as you would have to do in imperative UIKit.
